Connection & dynamism

24 screens and 4 motion studies on how FleetOS entities connect: jobs with drivers, jobs with trucks, drivers with documents, trucks with documents, trucks with restrictions, and everything together. Pushed further than the research pack (dark canvases, editorial type, isometric planes, boards), while still ink on paper.
