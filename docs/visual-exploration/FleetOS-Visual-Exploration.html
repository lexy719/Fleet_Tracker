How the studies were made and judged

One world, many questions

Every study shows the same moment: Miguel Costa (D-14) on the A1 at km 282 with AA-42-ZP + PT-3821, a pause due by 10:50, Coimbra 12:30–13:30, Porto 15:30–17:00, the VCI rule with one unknown fact, and seven other transports. Only the composition changes, so comparisons are fair.

Variety enforced

No map / tiny / 25% / 50% / portrait / corridor / fullscreen / map with embedded axis; horizontal, vertical and km×time planes; object-centred, edge, bottom and margin inspectors; typography-only states. Whenever three studies drifted toward content-left / map-right, the next one changed family.

Picked from the environment (E-series)

14 existing lab states (5.7K / 5.7L captures) were judged by the same rules, so the current direction competes on equal terms with the new ideas.

Verdicts

KEEPA strong FleetOS direction worth integrating as a whole composition.
MAYBEOne or more strong ideas, but not a complete composition.
REJECTNot suitable for FleetOS (wrong form, decorative, generic, or misleading).

Harshness

The first pass produced 30 KEEP out of 59, which wasn't critical enough. A second pass downgraded anything that was a layer rather than a view, duplicated a better study, depended on data FleetOS doesn't own, or framed Portugal in landscape. Final: 15 KEEP · 31 MAYBE · 27 REJECT.

Values held constant

Off-white surfaces, ink selection, restrained colour (risk amber, blocker red, unknown dashed grey), strong type, technical tone. No neon, glass, gradients, KPI cards or decorative motion.
