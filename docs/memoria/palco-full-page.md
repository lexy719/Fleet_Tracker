A palco must always be the focus and take the whole page. Never share the screen half and half with the map, whether as a map band on top with lanes below or as a split screen with a white panel.

**Why:** on 2026-10-19 the "O dia" mockup (map band on top, lanes below) was called horrible: "um palco tem que ser sempre o foco, tem que ser sempre a página inteira". The split "Quem leva o quê" panel was already "demasiado simples".

**How to apply:** switching to a palco replaces the map entirely. The map's things morph into the palco (pings → lanes, etc.). To get the map back you switch palco again (e.g. tap a lane → full-screen map on that route), as in the palcos idea reel. Related: [[fleetos-cinema-direction]], [[show-before-build]]
